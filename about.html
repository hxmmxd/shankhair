Our Story

From Tempo Driver
to Airline Owner

An inspiring story of entrepreneurship and determination. Our exclusive low-fare routes with exceptional savings are designed to make your journey comfortable, reliable and accessible to every Indian — regardless of their background.

In this exclusive journey, our founder set out with a vision of making air travel accessible to every Indian. From humble beginnings to launching an airline, the story behind Shankh Air is one of grit, passion, and an unrelenting belief that affordable flying can change lives.

Shankh Air will connect major cities within and outside Uttar Pradesh, boosting regional connectivity and economic growth. Initial routes include Lucknow, Varanasi, Gorakhpur, and key metros like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru.
